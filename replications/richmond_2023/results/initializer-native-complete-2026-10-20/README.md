These reproduce earlier released behavior; they are not newly discovered independent cases.

The paper and source disagree about maximum metabolism, capacity, and distance geometry, as documented in preceding source and landscape audits.
This experiment measures those alternatives without resolving which historical interpretation produced the published results.
Observed destinations condition state accounting; GPU random choices, conflict winners, and within-step scheduling are not independently reconstructed.
Update order and broader population/scaling behavior remain qualifications. This single condition does not establish a corrected full-grid result or whole-paper completion.
