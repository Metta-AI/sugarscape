## Fixed publication defect

Dogan's published figure directory had flattened PNG paths and omitted the canonical CSV from the layout required by its unchanged script.
The original files already existed elsewhere in the committed study and matched their source hashes.
[Study PR 11](https://app.graphite.dev/github/pr/Metta-AI/sugarscape/11) restores the complete `results/canonical/` subtree and documents the directly executable audit.
No simulation, parameter, seed, native script, plotted value or comparison tolerance changed.

Initial review invocations also exposed environment/input-selection mistakes: a missing Mesa dependency, an analysis environment without pandas, and an incomplete Oremland source directory.
The retained pinned environments and sealed complete inputs resolved those invocation failures; no scientific result was substituted.
Lima's current auditor adds native NetLogo creation checks and a narrower statement about unobserved sugar draws.
Its report therefore has additional fields and revised scope text; all prior measurement fields remain identical.
The earlier report and the failed whole-report equality assumption remain retained rather than being silently relabeled as identical.

## Coworld feasibility

The existing Coworld passes 636 functional tests with five reported skips, plus its one serial performance test.
Its current five-variant template also passes the versioned platform manifest reader after supplying build-time version/image placeholders.
These checks do not establish hosted certification or implement the new research adapters.

The proposed collection uses named variants and native study adapters, with explicit reference and playable modes.
CPU families can share a world; the CUDA study may require a linked GPU companion because variant configs do not override game resources.
See the [Coworld design PR](https://app.graphite.dev/github/pr/Metta-AI/coworld-sugarscape/45) for the per-study mapping and implementation gates.
