# Sugarscape replication program

The recorded program completed ten qualified method studies. Exact numerical agreement and method completion remain distinct.

Sources: [publication 1](https://www.jasss.org/25/2/1.html), [publication 2](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3519139).

| Study | Completed scope and principal qualification | PR |
| --- | --- | --- |
| McCulloch 2022 | Calibration and uncertainty workflow; mixed stabilization and posterior results, historical seeds unavailable. | [3](https://app.graphite.dev/github/pr/Metta-AI/sugarscape/3) |
| Carrella 2021 | 5,000 author and 5,000 independent simulations; all 75 finite estimator workflows. Thirty-eight printed matches and thirty-seven discrepancies remain. | [4](https://app.graphite.dev/github/pr/Metta-AI/sugarscape/4) |
| Dogan 2026 | Completed mixed working-paper study; retained discrepancies and explicit scope, not a peer-reviewed publication. | [11](https://app.graphite.dev/github/pr/Metta-AI/sugarscape/11) |
| Izquierdo 2009 | Positive structural replication within its declared Sugarscape scope; no broader historical identity inferred. | [14](https://app.graphite.dev/github/pr/Metta-AI/sugarscape/14) |
| Quera 2025 | Sugarscape gradients, both 5,000-epoch calibration arms and predictive assessment. Reconstructed settings and VarGrad mismatch prevent a historical or validated-negative verdict. | [15](https://app.graphite.dev/github/pr/Metta-AI/sugarscape/15) |
| Thaler 2019 | Qualified simulation and benchmark method. Separate failed property-suite evidence and historical timing limitations remain explicit. | [16](https://app.graphite.dev/github/pr/Metta-AI/sugarscape/16) |
| Richmond 2023 | All 84 original conditions and figure analyses. Author-code defects and historical hardware/image limitations remain explicit. | [18](https://app.graphite.dev/github/pr/Metta-AI/sugarscape/18) |
| Lima 2024 | Both native distributed-agent scenarios, saved-state/GUI and conservation checks. Historical binary equivalence is unproven. | [19](https://app.graphite.dev/github/pr/Metta-AI/sugarscape/19) |
| Oremland 2014 | Sugarscape convergence, reduction, ranking, optimization, held-out transfer and timing. Independent reconstruction with qualified historical settings. | [21](https://app.graphite.dev/github/pr/Metta-AI/sugarscape/21) |
| Brearcliffe 2020/2021 | All 1,600 native runs, both parameter interpretations and claim analyses. Mixed endpoint agreement; recorder correction and unavailable historical seeds documented. | [23](https://app.graphite.dev/github/pr/Metta-AI/sugarscape/23) |

Only Izquierdo has a wholly positive verdict under its declared structural scope. Dogan is an unpublished working paper. The other study PRs retain their incomplete or qualified findings.
